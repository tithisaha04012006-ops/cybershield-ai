Next-Gen Defense Capabilities

Built for Modern Web Security

Traditional blacklists react days after attackers launch malicious domains. CyberShield AI analyzes structural attack patterns at wire speed.

Raw IP & Hostname Interception

Instantly flags URLs connecting directly to bare IPv4/IPv6 endpoints or octal/hex obfuscations commonly deployed in phishing kits and botnet staging.

SSL / TLS Transport Inspection

Verifies transport layer encryption and penalizes unencrypted plain HTTP links where credentials, cookies, and payment details are exposed to MitM interception.

Typosquatting & Punycode Defense

Identifies visual homoglyphs (e.g. Cyrillic "а" vs Latin "a", leetspeak like "paypa1" or "goog1e") and Punycode "xn--" internationalized domain deceptions.

Lexical & Social Engineering Radar

Extracts credential-harvesting tokens, financial bait, fake lottery claims, and emergency account-lock phrases stacked across subdomains and deep URI paths.

Shannon Entropy Anomaly Profiling

Computes mathematical randomness across domain strings to identify Domain Generation Algorithms (DGA) used by ransomware families and malware command servers.

Explainable Trust & Risk Index

No black-box opacity. CyberShield AI calculates a transparent 0-100 score with itemized impact points so security analysts and users understand why a link is risky.

Architectural Pipeline

How CyberShield AI Analyzes URLs

From the moment a link is submitted, our modular detection pipeline evaluates multiple threat dimensions in milliseconds.

01 Ingestion

Syntax & Normalization

Parses scheme, port, host authority, path fragments, and query parameters. Validates structure and identifies obfuscated encoding techniques.

02 Inspection

Heuristic Extraction

Inspects protocol security, checks TLD risk profiles, detects naked IP addresses, and flags homoglyphic lookalike character substitutions.

03 Synthesis

Risk Weighting

Assigns mathematical penalties across structural vectors, weighs brand safety reputations, and calculates a normalized 0-100 risk score.

04 Advisory

Digital Trust Advisory

Delivers classified assessment (Low, Medium, or High Risk) paired with actionable protective guidance and audit trails stored in SQLite.
